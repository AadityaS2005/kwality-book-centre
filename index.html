About the Store

Kwality Book Centre — Premier Bookstore in Lodhi Colony, New Delhi

Situated in the vibrant Meharchand Market at 7, Lodhi Road, Lodhi Colony, New Delhi (110003), Kwality Book Centre has served as South Delhi's dependable academic and competitive exam bookstore since 2005. Whether you are a student preparing for board exams or an aspirant preparing for civil services, our store provides an extensive inventory of over 5,000 titles.

📚 School Books (Classes 1–12)

Complete curriculum books for CBSE, ICSE, ISC, and Delhi State Board. Official NCERT textbooks and solutions available for all subjects including Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Accountancy, and Economics.

🎯 Competitive Exam Books

Latest 2025 editions for UPSC Civil Services (Prelims & Mains), SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, IBPS PO, SBI Clerk, Railway RRB, CDS, NDA, JEE Mains, and NEET from top publishers like Arihant, Disha, and McGraw Hill.

⚡ Spiral & Hard Cover Binding

In-house document binding lab offering same-day spiral binding for college projects and university-standard hard-cover thesis binding with gold-foil embossed titles.

✏️ Stationery & Gifting

Full range of school and office stationery from Staedtler, Camlin, Pilot, and Classmate, alongside occasion-themed book gift wrapping for birthdays and prize distributions.

Conveniently located near Jor Bagh Metro Station (Yellow Line) and Khan Market Metro Station (Violet Line), our store is open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Call or WhatsApp us at 095822 62883 for stock availability or inquiries.
