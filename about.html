Our Story & Heritage

Built on a Passion for Learning & Community

Founded in 2005 at 7, Lodhi Road, Meharchand Market, Kwality Book Centre was started with a fundamental promise: to provide students and parents in South Delhi with authentic, high-quality books at 100% fair and transparent prices.

Over the past 20 years, we have grown into one of Delhi’s most relied-upon academic hubs. From young children picking up their first NCERT & CBSE textbooks to determined aspirants preparing for the UPSC Civil Services & SSC exams, we support learners through every milestone of their academic journey.

Unlike online platforms where editions are uncertain, every book at Kwality Book Centre is a verified, authentic publisher print with up-to-date syllabi.

Our Journey

20 Years of Milestones

How Kwality Book Centre evolved from a neighbourhood bookstore into South Delhi’s academic cornerstone.

2005
Kwality Book Centre Opens at Meharchand Market
Opened at 7 Lodhi Road with a dedicated focus on CBSE & NCERT school textbooks and basic student stationery for Lodhi Colony residents.
2008
Competitive Exam Books Division
Expanded into UPSC Civil Services, SSC, and Banking exam prep books as demand from civil service aspirants in South Delhi grew rapidly.
2012
Professional Binding Services Lab
Launched in-house spiral binding and thesis hard-cover binding. Became the primary binding partner for students from Lady Shri Ram, JNU, and Delhi University.
2016
Premium Stationery & Gifting Section
Introduced imported stationery brands (Staedtler, Pilot) and occasion-themed gift wrapping for books and student awards.
2022
WhatsApp Order & Stock Check Service
Introduced instant WhatsApp-based catalogue enquiries, allowing students to check stock and reserve titles before travelling to the store.
2025
Celebrating 20 Years of Community Service
Over 10,000 students served, 112+ 5-star reviews, and continuous daily operations at 7, Lodhi Road, Meharchand Market, New Delhi.
