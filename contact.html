Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you stock UPSC & SSC 2025 preparation books? ▾

Yes! We regularly update stock with latest UPSC books (Laxmikanth, Spectrum, Vision IAS), SSC CGL/CHSL books from Arihant and Disha, and all major competitive exam titles. Call 095822 62883 to check a specific title's availability.

How fast is your spiral binding service? ▾

Most spiral binding orders are completed in 30–60 minutes. Walk in, hand us your pages, and collect the same day. For large bulk orders (50+ copies), call ahead and we'll prioritise your order.

Do you offer bulk discounts for school book orders? ▾

Yes — we offer institutional and bulk pricing for schools, colleges, and organisations. WhatsApp or call us with your book list and quantity and we'll send you a custom quote.

Which Metro station is nearest to your store? ▾

Jor Bagh Metro Station (Yellow Line) is the closest — about 5 minutes walking. Khan Market (Violet Line) is 8 minutes away. We are inside Meharchand Market, 7 Lodhi Road, Lodhi Colony.

Do you do thesis and dissertation hard binding? ▾

Yes — we specialise in premium hard-cover case binding for theses, dissertations, and official reports. Embossed cover titles, multiple colour choices, and university submission standard quality. Call or WhatsApp for pricing.

Can I check book stock before visiting? ▾

Yes! Just WhatsApp us with the book title and author. We'll check and confirm availability within a few hours, saving you the trip if we're out of stock.
